Overview

Research assistant at the UW EMIT (Emerging Mixed-signal Integrated circuit Technology) Lab, working on a Mixed Signal Simulated Bifurcation Machine (SBM) — a hardware accelerator for solving combinatorial optimization problems.

Simulated Bifurcation is an algorithm inspired by quantum adiabatic optimization that maps Ising-model problems to a network of coupled oscillators. Implementing this in mixed-signal hardware allows for significant speed and energy advantages over purely digital or software-based solvers.

This research sits at the intersection of analog circuit design, digital control, and computational theory — directly aligned with interests in mixed-signal device design.

Focus Areas

Mixed-signal implementation of coupled oscillator networks, analog compute primitives for optimization, and hardware/algorithm co-design for Ising-model solvers.
