Overview

A multi-year Engineers Without Borders project to design and deploy accessible wheelchair and mobility device charging stations across the UW Seattle campus. The goal is to remove a practical barrier for students and visitors who rely on powered mobility devices.

The project involved identifying unmet campus needs, designing a cost-effective charging solution that surpasses existing options, and navigating state university regulations for installing electrical equipment in public spaces.
